Introducing Hamptons Fieldhouse

A Premier Multisport Facility in the Heart of the Riverhead.

Strategically located with easy access for neighboring towns including the Riverhead, The Northfork, Hamptons Bays, Westhampton Beach, East Quogue, and Southampton, this state-of-the-art facility promises a haven for sports enthusiasts of all ages.

What We Offer

From sports leagues and training programs to special events and celebrations, Hamptons Fieldhouse brings our community together. Our versatile space welcomes soccer, lacrosse, baseball, pickleball, yoga, and more—with room for birthday parties, sports camps, and athletes of all ages to play, train, and grow.
