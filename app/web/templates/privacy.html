HAMPTONS FIELDHOUSE

Privacy & Cookies

Last updated October 23, 2026

Our commitment to your privacy

Hamptons Fieldhouse uses your information to respond to you, manage your account, and provide bookings and services. We do not sell or rent your personal information, or share it with third parties for their marketing or advertising. Limited processing by the providers that operate our services is explained below.

Information we collect and store

  • Contact requests: first and last name, email address, phone number, and the message you submit.
  • Accounts: name, email, phone number, account identifier, confirmation status, and authentication information. Supabase handles password authentication; passwords are not stored in your public profile.
  • Bookings: contact details, selected service, dates and times, party size, extras, food preferences, notes, prices, payment method and plan, and booking and payment status. This website does not collect card numbers or bank account details.
  • Camp and clinic registrations: parent or guardian contact details, participant name and grade, selected session and price, and confirmation that the participant waiver was completed. Square processes payment on its secure checkout page; this website does not collect card numbers or bank account details.
  • Technical information: our hosting and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, browser and device information, request times, and security or error logs when you visit.
  • Browser preferences: acknowledgment of our privacy notice and the accessibility settings you choose.

Please avoid including payment credentials, medical details, or other sensitive information in messages or booking notes.

How we use information

We use information to answer questions, authenticate accounts, coordinate rentals and registrations, track booking and payment status, provide customer service, and protect the website against errors and misuse.

Service providers and disclosures

When you open Square checkout, Square provides the card-entry form and processes payment information, billing contact details, shipping details for merchandise, and technical connection information. Our server sends Square the items and prices needed to create checkout and stores payment references and results for bookings; it does not receive or store your full card number.

Supabase processes account authentication and stores contact, profile, and booking records. Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes website requests. Printful supplies merchandise catalog information and product images and receives paid merchandise orders and shipping details through the connected Square store for fulfillment. Visiting the Merch page may connect your browser to Printful’s image service. Providers receive information needed to support these services. Authorized staff access customer information to manage requests, bookings, and orders. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect safety, prevent fraud, or defend legal rights.

Google Fonts supplies the fonts used on this site and receives technical connection information such as your IP address. The embedded Google Map loads automatically when you visit our Contact page, without requiring a separate click. This connects your browser to Google, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser or device information and may use its own cookies. Google handles this information under its privacy policy.

Links to waivers, maps, social media, or payment services take you to services with their own privacy practices. Information submitted there is handled under their policies. See Supabase’s privacy policy, Vercel’s privacy policy, Printful’s privacy policy, and Google’s privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

We use essential sign-in cookies: fh_access lasts for the authentication session’s configured lifetime, and fh_refresh lasts up to 14 days and can be renewed when your session refreshes. These cookies are HttpOnly, so page scripts cannot read them. Signing out clears them from this browser.

Local storage entries fh_privacy_notice_v1 and fh_accessibility_v1 remember your notice acknowledgment and accessibility preferences until you clear site data or reset the applicable settings. They do not contain account credentials. If browser storage is blocked, choices may not persist between pages.

We do not add advertising or analytics tools to this site. Third-party services, including the automatically loaded Google Map, may use their own cookies under their policies. The first-visit notice explains these practices. You can reopen it using “Cookie notice” in the footer. Your browser can delete or block cookies and site data; blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in and booking.

Retention and security

We keep records as needed to manage your account, bookings, customer requests, and applicable recordkeeping or dispute-resolution obligations. Retention can vary by record type, and backups or legally required records may remain after a deletion request. Contact us to ask about a particular record.

We use authenticated access and restricted staff access to help protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You can update your name and phone number in your account. To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, or ask about privacy rights available where you live, email hamptonsfieldhouse@gmail.com or call (631) 278-4374. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some records may need to be retained for legal or operational obligations.

Children and family bookings

Parents or guardians should manage accounts and bookings for children. Please do not submit a child’s personal information beyond what is needed to arrange a service. If you believe a child has provided information without appropriate parent or guardian involvement, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its revision date when our practices change, and provide additional notice when appropriate. Contact Hamptons Fieldhouse at hamptonsfieldhouse@gmail.com, (631) 278-4374, or 605 Old Country Rd., Riverhead, NY 11901.
