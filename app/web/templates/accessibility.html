Accessibility

Helping everyone access our services.

Our accessibility commitment

Hamptons Fieldhouse is committed to welcoming people with disabilities and improving access to our website and services. We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) as a reference for improvements in support of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

Customize your experience

The Accessibility button in the bottom-right corner opens options for larger text, higher contrast, underlined links, and reduced motion. Settings are saved only in this browser. Reset settings restores the defaults; your device’s reduced-motion preference still applies.

You can also use your browser’s zoom controls, navigate with Tab and Shift+Tab, activate buttons with Enter or Space, and use the “Skip to content” link at the beginning of each page. Press Escape to close the accessibility menu. With JavaScript disabled, this statement and the contact options below remain available.

Need assistance or found a barrier?

If a page, form, calendar, or third-party service is difficult to use, we can help with facility information and booking requests. Call (631) 278-4374 or email hamptonsfieldhouse@gmail.com. Our listed business hours are daily, 6 AM–11 PM.

When reporting an issue, include the page address, what you were trying to do, and your preferred way for us to respond. Browser or assistive-technology details are helpful but optional. You do not need to disclose a diagnosis.

Ongoing improvements

These reading tools support individual preferences; they are not a certification that every page or external service meets every accessibility requirement. We welcome feedback to help identify and fix barriers. For facility access or accommodation questions, please contact us before your visit so we can discuss your needs.
